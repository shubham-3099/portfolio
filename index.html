01 / THE PERSON

Understand it. Then question it.

I'm Shubham Sharma, a computer science graduate from India with a fascination for how systems work and where their assumptions break.

My ambition is to become the world's number one vulnerability researcher. That's the destination I'm working toward, not a title I'm claiming today.

I want to develop the kind of understanding that lets me look beyond the obvious: read the code, trace the behaviour, ask better questions, and find something that everyone else missed.

This is my place on the internet. A small introduction to a much bigger journey.

THE APPROACH Curiosity. Patience. Depth.
02 / THE PURSUIT

The work before the discovery.

Great discoveries begin with a deep understanding of ordinary things.

I'm building that understanding from the ground up: operating systems, networks, programming, and the way software behaves beneath its interface.

  1. 01

    Build the foundations

    Understand Linux, networking, and programming through practice and explanation.

  2. 02

    Learn to investigate

    Read code, reproduce vulnerabilities in labs, and learn to explain their root causes.

  3. 03

    Make an original contribution

    Work toward discovering new vulnerabilities and reporting them responsibly.

A PRINCIPLE TO KEEP Understand the system, not just the tool.
03 / THE WORLD

A passport. An open mind.

I want this journey to take me into the world, not just deeper into a screen.

I want to travel, meet hackers from different countries, and spend time with people who approach the same problem in completely different ways.

The plan is to learn from conversations as much as from books: conference hallways, late-night discussions, and the unexpected connections that come from showing up.

“The world is bigger than my current understanding of it.”

For now, that's an ambition I'm building toward. There are still a lot of firsts ahead.
